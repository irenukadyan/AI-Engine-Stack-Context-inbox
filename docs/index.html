One short path from phone to project

No emailing yourself, downloading on another device, or finding the right project folder by hand.

01 · OPEN

Open your project

Start VS Code as usual. Context Inbox recognizes the folders currently open in your workspace.

02 · PAIR

Scan the QR code

Use your phone camera once to open the private upload page on your local network.

03 · SEND

Send context

Choose the project, attach a screenshot, PDF, file, or short instruction, then send.

04 · BUILD

Continue coding

Your context arrives in a visible project folder, ready for you or your coding agent to review.
