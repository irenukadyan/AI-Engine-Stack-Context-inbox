# Client Proposal Outline

1. Executive summary — client problem, desired outcome, and recommended approach.
2. Our understanding — current state, goals, and constraints.
3. Proposed solution — scope, key journeys, and high-level architecture.
4. Delivery plan — phases, milestones, responsibilities, and indicative timeline.
5. Investment — selected price option, payment schedule, taxes, and third-party costs.
6. Assumptions and exclusions — plain, visible, and specific.
7. Change control — how additional scope is evaluated and approved.
8. Warranty, support, and post-launch options.
9. Next steps — approval, discovery workshop, or contract.
